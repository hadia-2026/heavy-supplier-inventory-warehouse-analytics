\# Heavy Supplier, Inventory \& Warehouse Analytics



\## Project Overview



This project is part of the CodeTX Data Science Virtual Work Experience.



The goal of this project is to analyze supplier, product, inventory, warehouse, customer, sales, purchasing, and stock movement data in order to support better supply-chain decisions.



The project focuses on identifying patterns, risks, and opportunities related to inventory health, warehouse performance, supplier reliability, customer behavior, and future demand.



\## Role



Data Scientist



\## Duration



12 Weeks



\## Project Objectives



\- Understand product movement and demand patterns

\- Measure inventory health and stock levels

\- Identify fast-moving and slow-moving products

\- Evaluate warehouse operational performance

\- Analyze supplier reliability and procurement risk

\- Study customer purchasing behavior

\- Detect stock and operational risks

\- Develop predictive models for demand and inventory planning

\- Build meaningful business dashboards

\- Provide data-driven recommendations
